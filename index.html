Standing rule

Mindset

Judge decisions, not outcomes

Process over scoreline

A death from a well-timed aggressive read is a correct decision even if it loses the round. A survival from over-holding a passive angle can be wrong even if it wins. Grade each round on whether the call made sense with the information you had.

"Was that a good read, or did it just work out?" — ask this after every death, win or lose.

Catch it before it compounds

Tilt tells

Your data already shows the pattern — a 0.9 K/D loss into a 0.4 K/D loss back to back (Sep 13→14). The tells usually show up before the scoreline does:

  • Forcing a duel to "make up for" your last death
  • Holding a grudge against one specific enemy across rounds
  • Typing in chat instead of calling in voice/comms

Any one of these mid-game is a cue to use the hard stop rule early.

Separate identity from role fit

Reframe Duelist losses

Controller and Initiator win rates (50.4%, 50.7%) beat Duelist (44.9%) by a wide margin. A rough game on Reyna isn't evidence you're bad — it's evidence you picked a role that statistically doesn't fit your playstyle as well.

Reduce variance, not just raise the ceiling

Pre-round routine as an anchor

MVP games sitting next to sub-1.0 K/D losses means your ceiling is fine — your floor is the problem. A fixed pre-round routine (same crosshair check, same breath, same "what's my role this round" question) raises the floor more than any single mechanical fix.

Autopilot habits

  • Say your round plan out loud before it starts
  • Stand up, water, look away — every 2 games, no exceptions
  • Vary the warm-up scenario order occasionally so the warm-up itself doesn't go on autopilot

Workflow

During session
Run off this page standalone. No need to loop Claude in — it's built to be self-sufficient in the moment.
After each game
Send the log — agent, W/L, K/D, one mistake. This is the only way Claude sees what happened; the more consistent the logs, the more patterns surface (like the Sep 13→14 tilt chain).
Weekly
Send the last-20 tracker.gg screenshot. Trend gets checked against baseline and map/benchmark priorities get adjusted if they're stale.
Monthly
Full profile + benchmark re-pull. Agent pool and role split get reassessed, and this page gets rewritten if priorities shifted.
Anytime
Live questions — hold or push this round, matchup calls, a VOD clip you want a second opinion on. This is where a live chat adds the most value over a static page.
Worth turning on Memory in Claude's settings — without it, every new chat starts blank and won't recall last week's log. With it on, trend tracking across the whole grind gets much easier.

Honest note on the climb: Platinum to Immortal is usually a multi-month grind decided by consistency, not volume. The data already shows quality drops hard after 2 losses in a row — sticking to the 3-game cap even when behind on rank progress gets there faster than pushing to 6–7 games on tilt.
